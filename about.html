Our Story

DLS Services was founded with a simple purpose: to make homes, rental properties and businesses cleaner, more comfortable and more functional.

What started as a passion for helping people maintain their spaces has grown into a company serving Orlando, Florida and nearby areas with professional cleaning, handyman and remodeling services — built around professionalism, integrity, responsibility and care.
